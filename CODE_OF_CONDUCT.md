# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or any other personal characteristic.

All contributors and participants are expected to interact respectfully and professionally.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using respectful and welcoming language.
- Being considerate of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback politely.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Showing empathy and professionalism toward other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately offensive comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual or inappropriate attention.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior.

They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, including the project's repository, issue discussions, pull requests, and other project-related activities.

It may also apply when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed and investigated appropriately. Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy and safety of people who report incidents.

Appropriate action may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restriction of participation, or removal from project activities.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will consider the circumstances and seriousness of each violation when determining an appropriate response.

Responses should be fair, proportional to the violation, and consistent with the goals of maintaining a respectful project environment.
